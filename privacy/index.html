Privacy Policy

Stify Notes · Last updated November 11, 2026

HomeSupport

Stify Notes is made by Erdinç Ersoy ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Stify Notes app for iPhone.

The short version

We do not collect, store, sell or share any of your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your notes

Everything you write — notes, lists, links, categories, reminders and settings — is stored on your iPhone.

If you buy Stify Pro and turn on iCloud sync, your notes are also stored in your own private iCloud database, provided by Apple. Only you can access it; we cannot see, read or recover it. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud: apple.com/legal/privacy.

You can delete any note at any time. Deleting the app removes the notes stored on the device. Notes synced to iCloud can be removed in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or anyone else.

Purchases

Stify Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether Stify Pro has been purchased on your Apple Account.

Widgets and links

The widget shows your latest notes on your iPhone only. Links you add open in Safari or the app they belong to; Stify Notes does not track which links you open.

Category suggestions

Category suggestions are worked out on your iPhone from the words and links in your note. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Children

Stify Notes does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open a request on our help page.
