Need help or found a bug?

Open a request on our help page and we will get back to you. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Contact support

Requests are public, so please don't include personal information or the contents of private notes.

How do I write a note?

Tap the yellow button at the bottom right of the home screen. The note keeps the date and time it was created.

How do to-do lists work?

While writing, tap the list button and choose To-do list or Creature to-do list. Tap a box (or a character's head) to tick an item; tap again to untick it.

How do I add bold, italic or a link?

While writing, select some text and tap B, I or the link button above the keyboard. With nothing selected, bold and italic apply to what you type next.

Reminders don't show up

Check that notifications are allowed: iPhone Settings → Notifications → Stify Notes. Reminders are not delivered while the phone is in a Focus mode that silences Stify Notes.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the home screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Stify Notes and choose a size. On iOS 18 and later you can also add a Quick note button to Control Center.

What does Stify Pro include?

Unlimited notebooks, all 12 characters for lists and to-dos, unlimited categories and reminders, and iCloud sync between your iPhones. It is a one-time purchase.

How do I restore Stify Pro on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings → Stify Pro in the app and tap Restore purchase.

How does iCloud sync work?

With Stify Pro, turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings. Your notes are stored in your own iCloud and appear on your other iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account. The change takes effect the next time you open the app.

Can I change the language?

Yes. Settings → Language. With System language the app follows your iPhone.
